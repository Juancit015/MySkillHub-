## Overview

Emil Kowalski (creator of the Sonner toast library, ex-Vercel and Linear) packaged years of product design and motion engineering experience into 10 portable skills. They cover the gap where an agent-building-UI misses the invisible details that make an interface feel right — touch targets, spring curves, when a motion is gratuitous vs. earned.

## Skills included

1. **emil-design-eng** — the flagship: animation + design-taste guidance.
2. **animate** — build an animation from scratch, making the right decisions in order.
3. **review-animations** — critique motion code against a high craft bar.
4. **improve-animations** — audit a whole codebase's motion, produce a fix roadmap.
5. **find-animation-opportunities** — spots where motion should exist but doesn't.
6. **animation-vocabulary** — reverse-lookup glossary for vague motion descriptions.
7. **apple-design** — Apple's design + motion philosophy, translated for the web.
8. **pick-ui-library** — curated, opinionated library picker for common frontend tasks.
9. **prototype** — build multiple genuinely different versions behind a picker.
10. **ask-sonner** — guide for wiring up and styling the Sonner toast library.
