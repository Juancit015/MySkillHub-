## Overview

A meta-skill: its job is not to do your work, but to watch you do yours and turn what it learned into *better other skills*. When you correct the agent, change a workflow, or apply a piece of judgment, task-observer captures it and folds it back into your existing skills (or creates new ones). Domain-agnostic — works with any other skill in your stack.
