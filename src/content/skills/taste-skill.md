## Overview

Leon Lin's Taste-Skill is a 12-skill collection that gives an agent "good taste" across frontend work — design, UX, redesign, image-to-code, mobile design, and more. It includes specialized variants (`gpt-taste` for Codex/GPT, `image-to-code`, `redesign`) and explicit styles (brutalist, minimalist, soft), with three tunable dials: `DESIGN_VARIANCE`, `MOTION_INTENSITY` and `VISUAL_DENSITY`, each on a 1-10 scale. Framework-agnostic (React, Vue, Svelte).

Strong thematic overlap with Impeccable — both target the generic-AI-design-slop problem.
